A 100-word monologue for Lady Morvain, a fantasy villain who drowned the fleet at Saltspire and wears the Hollow Bell — a relic that rings when liars speak. She greets the hero sent to slay her, deflects into stew and unanswered letters from her rival Anselm, loses the thread mid-sentence, and lands the sting in the last line. Her exhaustion is never announced; it lives in the pauses, the trailing thoughts, and the small domestic deflections, while the menace never leaves the room.
